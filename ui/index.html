Help & privacy

Welcome to Flyover.

  1. Choose an area and planning date.
  2. Explore a course and check availability on its official website.
  3. After you play, save your score and review the experience in Rounds.

Accounts and sync

On the private synced site, sign in with the same ChatGPT account on each device. On the older Pages site, scores stay in that browser. Export them there and import them in Rounds on the synced site.

Your information

Flyover stores the scores, reviews and profile fields you save in your account. Your sign-in email is displayed to you. Reviews are private to your account. Favorites and availability-check plans stay on this browser. No payment details are collected.

Export or delete

In Rounds, choose Export my data to download synced rounds, reviews and profile data. You can delete individual rounds or all your Flyover data. This does not delete your ChatGPT account. Deletion removes active app records; hosting-provider logs and backups may follow their own retention. Export before deleting anything you want to keep.

Reliability and privacy

Operational error logs contain a request reference and route category, not your score content or email. A per-account request counter helps protect the service and is retained separately from your golf data. We do not add advertising trackers or session recordings.

Need help?

For Flyover issues or data questions, email jbejarno@gmail.com. Include the error reference if one appears; do not send passwords. For reservations, refunds, cancellations or course conditions, contact the course directly.
